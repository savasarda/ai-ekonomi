## English Metadata

### App Name
AIEkonomi

### Subtitle
Family budget and cash flow tracker

### Promotional Text
Track shared expenses, recurring payments, income, portfolio value, and upcoming cash flow in one simple finance workspace.

### Description
AIEkonomi helps individuals and families follow everyday finances with a clear view of spending, income, recurring payments, portfolio value, and future cash flow.

Create family members, add cards or accounts, record expenses and income, manage subscriptions and debts, and see what is coming in the next days or months. The app is designed for practical money tracking, not financial advice.

Key features:
- Shared family budget tracking
- Income and expense records
- Recurring payment and debt tracking
- Cash flow view for 7, 30, 90 days, or custom dates
- Spending categories and monthly summaries
- Portfolio and savings overview
- Turkish and English language support
- TRY, USD, EUR, and GBP display support
- Privacy and data export tools

AIEkonomi does not provide investment, tax, credit, or legal advice. All records are for personal tracking and planning.
